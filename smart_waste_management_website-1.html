The project

Turn everyday waste into a resource.

This project proposes a practical system at OAV Paramanpur to understand school waste, separate it at source, recover useful materials and track progress with digital records.

🎯

Our aim

Develop a low-cost, digitally monitored waste management system for school.

🧪

Scientific approach

Identify waste sources, weigh waste, record observations and compare data.

🌿

Expected impact

Encourage segregation, composting, recycling and responsible habits.

How it works

Six steps. One cleaner campus.

01 · IDENTIFY

Find sources

Locate waste-generating areas.

02 · SEPARATE

Segregate

Use labelled bins.

03 · MEASURE

Weigh

Record quantities with a scale.

04 · RECOVER

Process

Compost suitable organics; recover recyclables.

05 · RECORD

Track

Enter data in a digital record.

06 · IMPROVE

Compare

Review trends and improve.

Safety & responsibility

Safe handling comes first.

Students should not handle medical, sanitary or hazardous waste. Composting and processing must be supervised by responsible adults. Keep personal information private and participation voluntary.
